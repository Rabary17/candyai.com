> **Short answer:** AI companions are safe for most adults who use them with limits. The main risks are emotional dependence, overspending on subscriptions and credits, privacy leaks and exposure of minors to adult content.

## The benefits people report

Users describe AI companions as a low-pressure place to talk, vent or practise conversation. **For some, they ease loneliness in difficult periods; for others, they are simply entertainment or interactive fiction.** They never tire, never judge and answer at any hour. <Todo>Add one or two recent studies with source and date.</Todo>

## Emotional dependence

These apps are designed to keep you engaged: instant replies, constant affection, reminders to come back. That design can lead some users to prefer the AI over people, or to feel distress when the app changes. **Warning signs** include skipping plans to chat, hiding usage, or feeling anxious when you cannot log in.

## Spending

Subscriptions are cheap on annual plans, but credits and tokens add up. On Candy AI, an image costs about 4 tokens and a call about 3 tokens per minute; heavy users can spend $25–$60 a month. Upgrade prompts often appear at emotional moments. Set a monthly cap before you start, as our [Candy AI pricing](/candy-ai/pricing/) page suggests.

## Privacy

Every message is stored on company servers, often without end-to-end encryption. Never share your full name, address, workplace, financial details or photos of real people. Check who runs the app and where; our page on [whether Candy AI is safe](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/) shows what to look for.

## Minors

Most romantic companion apps are adults-only, yet age checks are often a simple checkbox. Parents can use device controls, block domains at router level and talk openly about these apps. Watch for unexplained charges under company names such as "EverAI".
