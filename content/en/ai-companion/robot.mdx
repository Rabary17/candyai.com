An AI companion robot is a physical device that talks with you using artificial intelligence: a small desktop robot with a face on a screen, a robotic pet, or, rarely, a humanoid. **The body adds presence; the conversation still comes from the same kind of language model as the apps.**

## What they do well, and what they do not

Robots are good at routines: greetings, reminders, small talk, reacting with expressions. They are weaker at long, personal conversations, where memory and model quality matter more than hardware. Updates also depend on the maker staying in business.

## Privacy at home

A companion robot often has an always-on microphone and a camera. Check where recordings are processed, how long they are kept and whether you can turn sensors off.
