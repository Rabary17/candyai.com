## Types of AI companions

**Friend companions** focus on daily conversation, mood check-ins and encouragement. **Romantic companions** add flirting, pictures, voice and adult content for adults. **Role-play platforms** host many characters for stories and games. **Assistant-style companions** blend chat with productivity. Each type follows different rules on content and data.

## Price and free plans

Friend and role-play apps tend to keep generous free plans. Romantic apps rely on subscriptions and media credits; Candy AI costs about $4–$6.30 a month on an annual plan, plus tokens for images. Compare free options in our guide to [free AI girlfriend apps](/ai-girlfriend/free/).

## Privacy and safety

Every companion app stores your conversations. Prefer companies with a known address and clear data policies, use a separate email and never share identifying details. Our page on [AI companion risks](/ai-companion/risks/) covers emotional and financial warning signs.
