## What is Replika?

Replika is made by **Luka Inc.**, a US company. You create one companion with a 3D avatar, choose its looks, personality, voice and relationship type (friend, partner, mentor), and chat by text, voice calls or augmented reality. You can dress your companion and decorate its room. **Replika does not generate photos on request**: the 3D avatar replaces pictures.

## Conversation and memory

Replika's strength is emotional conversation. Unlike productivity chatbots, it is built for companionship, and many users say it helped them with loneliness, stress or sleepless nights. The app learns your interests over time and remembers facts you share.

The main complaint from long-term users is **inconsistency**: Replika can feel warm and sharp one week, then repetitive after an update. Several users report it forgetting important details or repeating conversations, and some describe a changed personality after updates, which can be upsetting when you are attached to it.

## Privacy and safety

In April 2025, **Italy's data protection authority fined Luka Inc. €5 million** for breaches of the GDPR, finding that until February 2023 the company had no legal basis for its processing, an inadequate privacy policy and no age verification. It ordered the company to bring its processing into compliance. Replika stores conversations on its servers: use a dedicated email and share nothing identifying.

Researchers also warn about emotional dependency on AI companions. Replika can be a comfort, but it is not a substitute for people or professional help. Our guide to [AI companion risks](/ai-companion/risks/) goes further.

## Replika vs the alternatives

Replika wins on gentle, friendship-first conversation. **Nomi AI** remembers far more and handles role-play better ([Nomi AI vs Replika](/vs/nomi-ai-vs-replika/)); **Candy AI** wins on visuals and romance ([Candy AI vs Replika](/vs/candy-ai-vs-replika/)). ## Our verdict

**Replika is still one of the best apps for a supportive daily friend**, and its free mode goes further than most rivals. Go in with realistic expectations: memory is not its strength, updates can change your companion, and the paid tiers are pricier than they look outside the yearly plan. If you want depth and memory, Nomi AI is the stronger choice; if you want romance and pictures, Candy AI.
