## The best free options

**Replika** remains the reference for a free daily friend, with check-ins and a calm tone. **Character AI** is free and unlimited, better for role-play than for a single companion. **Nomi AI** has a free tier with its strong memory. **Pi** and general assistants can listen, but do not keep a persona. ## When paying makes sense

If you hit the daily limit every day, a subscription usually costs less than a streaming service. Annual billing cuts the price by half on most apps. Before paying, read the cancellation steps and check what appears on your bank statement.
