## What makes an AI chat feel real?

Three things. **Context**: the reply builds on what you just said, not a template. **Memory**: personal details come back at the right moment. **Imperfection**: a believable character sometimes disagrees, asks questions or changes the subject. Apps that only flatter feel hollow within days.

## Text, voice notes or calls?

Text gives you time to think and costs nothing extra on most paid plans. Voice notes add warmth; calls feel closest to a real conversation but usually spend credits. On Candy AI, a call uses about 3 tokens per minute, as shown in our [Candy AI review](/candy-ai/).

## Tips for better conversations

- Give your character a clear backstory and personality in the creator.
- Correct it when it forgets: most apps update memory from your replies.
- Avoid one-word messages; richer input gives richer replies.
