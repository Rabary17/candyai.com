## Store app or web app?

Apple and Google take a cut of in-app purchases and ban explicit content. As a result, **many AI girlfriend services either skip the stores or ship a toned-down version there**. The web version, opened in Safari or Chrome and added to the home screen, usually has the full feature set and the lower price. Candy AI is a good example: see [is there a Candy AI app](/candy-ai/app/).
