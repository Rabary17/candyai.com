## What to look for in an anime AI girlfriend app

**An anime style toggle** in the creator, not just a filter. **Consistency** of face and outfit across pictures. **Writing** that fits the genre: expressions, tone and tropes without caricature. **Clear rules**: reputable apps enforce adult-only characters, and you should avoid any app that blurs that line.
