> **Short answer:** an AI girlfriend is an app where a chatbot plays a romantic partner. A language model writes the replies, a memory layer keeps facts about you, and optional image and voice models add pictures and calls. Most apps are freemium.

## How an AI girlfriend works

**A large language model** generates each reply from your message, the character's description and recent conversation. **A memory system** saves facts you share, like your job or your pet, and inserts them into later prompts. **Image models** draw the character on request, and **voice models** turn text into speech for notes and calls.

None of this involves feelings. The model predicts what a caring partner would plausibly say. That can feel warm and personal, and it is still a prediction.

## What AI girlfriends can and cannot do

They can hold long conversations at any hour, adapt to your tone, recall details and stay patient. They cannot meet you, share real experiences or make independent choices. Their "personality" depends on the app's design and rules, which can change with an update.

## How AI girlfriend apps make money

Most use freemium: a free plan with a cap, then a subscription. Many add a second currency, such as tokens or credits, for pictures, calls and video. Candy AI, for example, includes 100 tokens a month and sells packs on top; see [how Candy AI tokens work](/candy-ai/tokens/). Upgrade prompts often appear at emotional moments, which is worth knowing in advance.

## Who uses AI girlfriends?

Curious adults, people who feel lonely, people practising conversation, and users who enjoy interactive fiction. Motives vary, and so do outcomes. Some users find comfort; others notice they spend more time and money than planned. Our page on [AI companion risks](/ai-companion/risks/) covers both sides.

## How to try one sensibly

Start free, use a dedicated email, share nothing that identifies you, set a budget and keep an eye on how you feel. Our [free AI girlfriend](/ai-girlfriend/free/) guide lists apps you can test without paying, and our ranking of the [best AI girlfriend apps](/ai-girlfriend/) compares paid ones.
