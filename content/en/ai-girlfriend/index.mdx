## What makes a good AI girlfriend app?

Five things separate a good app from a frustrating one. **Conversation quality** comes first: natural replies, a stable personality and few repetitions. **Memory** decides whether the character still knows your job a week later. **Media** covers pictures, voice notes and calls. **Price transparency** matters because tokens and credits can triple a bill. **Safety** covers data handling and age checks.

## How much does an AI girlfriend cost?

Most apps charge $10–$25 a month on monthly billing and much less on annual plans. **The hidden cost sits in media credits.** Candy AI includes 100 tokens a month, about 25 images; others sell credits or cap images per day. Count your likely use before choosing, as our [Candy AI pricing breakdown](/candy-ai/pricing/) shows with three profiles.

## Using an AI girlfriend app in a healthy way

An AI girlfriend can be fun, comforting or simply curious. It is also designed to keep you coming back. Set a budget and a time limit, keep real-life connections, and never share identifying details. Our guide to [AI companion risks](/ai-companion/risks/) covers the warning signs.
