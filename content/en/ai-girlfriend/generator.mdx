What makes a character feel alive is the part people skip: personality, history and how she talks. This guide walks through both, using Candy AI as the example and noting where other apps differ.

## Step 2: design her look

Pick a style first: realistic or anime. Then set face, hair, eyes, body type and outfit. **Keep the description consistent**, because image generators use it as a reference for every future picture. <Todo>Screenshot of the Candy AI creator, cropped, SFW.</Todo>

## Step 3: give her a personality

Choose two or three core traits rather than ten: for example "playful, curious, a little sarcastic". Add her job, hobbies and the way she speaks. **Specific details beat adjectives**: "a nurse who loves bad horror films" gives the AI more to work with than "kind and fun".

## Step 4: write a backstory and your relationship

Describe how you met and what your relationship is: new date, long-term partner, friend. Two or three sentences are enough. This frame shapes every reply, and it helps the character stay consistent over weeks.

## Step 5: teach her about you

In the first conversations, share what you want her to remember: your name, job, interests. Correct her when she gets something wrong. Avoid real identifying details such as your surname or address, as explained in [is Candy AI safe](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/).

## Step 6: images and voice

Request a first picture to check the look, then adjust the creator if needed. On Candy AI each image costs about 4 tokens, so describe the scene precisely. Choose a voice that matches her personality before your first call.
