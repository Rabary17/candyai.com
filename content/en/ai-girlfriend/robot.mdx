## What a robot adds, and what it does not

A body adds presence: a face that reacts, a voice in the room. It does not add intelligence. **The conversation runs on the same kind of language model as the apps**, often with less memory and fewer updates. Robots also raise their own privacy questions, with cameras and microphones in your home.
