Every app on this site goes through the same protocol, so a score means the same thing from one review to the next.

## The protocol

- **One paid account per app**, bought with our own money.
- **<Todo>30</Todo> days of use**, following the same daily script: a 15-minute conversation, two image requests (when available) and one voice note.
- **Weekly**: a 10-minute call and a new character built from scratch.
- **Every token, credit and payment logged** in a spreadsheet.

## Scoring

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Conversation quality | 30% |
| Memory | 15% |
| Images | 20% |
| Voice | 10% |
| Value for money | 25% |

Apps without images are scored on the remaining criteria, rescaled to 10.

## What we never do

We never accept payment for a ranking position, publish explicit images, or review an app whose operating company we cannot identify.

## Updates

Prices are checked every month. Each app is fully retested every six months, or sooner after a major update.
