Every review on this site follows the same method, so a score means the same thing from one app to the next. We are transparent about where each fact comes from.

## Where our information comes from

- **Published tests.** Video and written reviews from the past 12 months, especially those that logged their spending or ran long conversations. We read full transcripts, not summaries.
- **User reports.** Trustpilot, Reddit and app-store reviews, used to spot recurring praise and complaints. One isolated post is never enough.
- **Official sources.** Each app's pricing page, help center, terms of service and privacy policy, checked on the date shown on the page.
- **Our own checks.** Where we can, we open a free account to check sign-up, free-plan limits and how upgrades are presented. A page only says "we checked" for things we actually checked ourselves.

A fact goes on a page only when **at least two independent sources agree**, or when it comes from the app's own official pages. When sources disagree, we say so.

## What we do not claim

Unless a review says otherwise, we have **not** run a paid month on that app ourselves. We never present other people's tests as our own, and we cite our sources at the bottom of each review. When we do run a paid test, the review says so and shows the dates and the money spent.

## Scoring

Our score is an editorial rating of the evidence, on the same weighted grid for every app:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Conversation quality | 30% |
| Memory | 15% |
| Images | 20% |
| Voice | 10% |
| Value for money | 25% |

Apps without images are scored on the remaining criteria, rescaled to 10.

## What we never do

We never accept payment for a ranking position, publish explicit images, or review an app whose operating company we cannot identify. Affiliate commissions never change a score.

## Updates

Prices are checked every month. Each review is revisited every six months, or sooner after a major update or a wave of user complaints.
