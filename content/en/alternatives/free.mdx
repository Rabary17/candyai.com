## Character AI — best free option for role-play

Character AI lets you chat with user-made characters for free, without a message cap in normal use. **The trade-off is strict filtering: no adult content and no romance beyond mild flirting.** It suits fans of anime, games and creative writing. A paid tier speeds up replies but is not needed. Full comparison in [Candy AI vs Character AI](/vs/candy-ai-vs-character-ai/).

## Replika — best free option for conversation

Replika's free plan covers text chat with a single companion in friend mode. **Romantic relationship status, voice calls and some activities require Replika Pro.** It is the most mature product for supportive, everyday conversation. See [Candy AI vs Replika](/vs/candy-ai-vs-replika/).

## Free vs paid: when the switch makes sense

If you hit a daily cap every day, or want images and voice, a paid plan becomes better value than juggling several free accounts. Candy AI's annual plan costs around $4–$6 a month. Before paying, read what the [Candy AI free plan](/candy-ai/free/) offers, or browse the full ranking of [Candy AI alternatives](/alternatives/candy-ai/).

## Avoid "free Premium" downloads

Mod APKs and "unlimited" clones of paid apps are not free versions: they are unofficial copies that may steal data. Every app in this list is used through its official site or store listing.
