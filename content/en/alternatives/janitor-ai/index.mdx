## Why people leave Janitor AI

- **Setup.** Getting good replies often means finding, configuring and paying for an external API.\n- **Uneven quality** from one model or character to the next.\n- **Downtime and queues** when traffic peaks.\n- **Privacy questions** when chats pass through third-party APIs; see [is Janitor AI safe](/alternatives/janitor-ai/is-it-safe/).

## Before you switch

Export or copy any character description you wrote on Janitor AI: most platforms let you paste it into their character creator. **Never download a modded APK that promises unlimited messages**: it is the most common malware trap in this niche.
