## How we tested

We ran the same role-play scenarios on PolyBuzz and on each alternative: a fantasy story, a slow-burn romance and a mystery with several characters, with memory checks after 50 and 200 messages. Scores follow our [test protocol](/how-we-test/).

## Before you switch

Export or copy any character description you wrote on PolyBuzz: most platforms let you paste it into their character creator. **Never download a modded APK that promises unlimited messages**: it is the most common malware trap in this niche.
