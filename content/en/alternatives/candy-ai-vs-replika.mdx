## Purpose and tone

**Replika, launched in 2017 by Luka Inc., positions itself as an AI friend.** It asks about your mood, suggests reflection exercises and keeps a diary of your exchanges. Romantic modes exist behind Pro, with tighter rules than Candy AI.

**Candy AI is built for romance and fantasy.** You design the partner's looks and personality, ask for pictures and unlock adult content on Premium. It rarely steers the talk toward your wellbeing.

## Images, voice and video

Candy AI generates pictures and Live Action clips of your character for tokens. Replika focuses on text, voice calls and an animated 3D avatar you can dress, but it does not generate photos of your companion on request.
