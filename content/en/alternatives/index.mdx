## Why people look for a Character AI alternative

- **The filter.** Romantic and adult themes are blocked, and the filter sometimes cuts in on violence in ordinary adventure stories.
- **Memory.** Characters forget names and events in long chats unless you repeat them.
- **Age rules.** Open-ended chat is closed to users under 18; adults also face more checks.
- **Speed and queues** at peak hours on the free plan.
