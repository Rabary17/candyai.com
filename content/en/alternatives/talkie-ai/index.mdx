## Why people leave Talkie

- **Ads and gems.** Free use is interrupted by ads, and the best features cost in-app currency.\n- **A strict filter**, designed for teenagers, that blocks romance and mature themes.\n- **Mobile first.** The experience is built for phones; web use is limited.\n- **Memory** that fades in long conversations.
- **Safety questions** from parents; see [is Talkie AI safe](/alternatives/talkie-ai/is-it-safe/).

## Before you switch

Export or copy any character description you wrote on Talkie: most platforms let you paste it into their character creator. **Never download a modded APK that promises unlimited messages**: it is the most common malware trap in this niche.
