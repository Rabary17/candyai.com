**Legal basis:** consent for analytics and affiliate tracking; legitimate interest for the age-gate cookie.

**Retention:** analytics data <Todo>14 months</Todo>; age-gate cookie 30 days.

**Your rights:** access, correction, deletion, objection and portability under GDPR. Contact us through the [contact page](/contact/). You can also complain to your data protection authority.

**Cookies:** a consent banner lets you accept or refuse non-essential cookies before any analytics or affiliate tracking loads.
