## Two different products

**Character AI is a platform of characters.** Users publish personas, from historical figures to anime heroes, and anyone can chat with them for free. Conversations are filtered, and the company has tightened its rules for minors. **Candy AI is a companion app.** You build one partner, or a few, and develop a relationship with images, calls and memory.

## Filters and adult content

Character AI's filter blocks explicit content, and there is no official setting to turn it off. Guides promising to bypass it break the terms and can get accounts banned. Candy AI allows adult content on paid plans for users who declare they are 18 or over.

## Images and voice

Character AI offers voice features but does not generate pictures of the character on demand. Candy AI does, for about 4 tokens per image, and adds calls and short videos.

## Price

Character AI is free, with an optional paid tier for speed and extras. Candy AI is free to try, then $13.99 a month or roughly $3.99–$6.30 a month on annual billing, plus token packs. If you want to stay free, see the [free Candy AI alternatives](/alternatives/free/).

Stay on **Character AI** for free, varied role-play without adult content. Move to **Candy AI** if you are an adult who wants one private, visual companion and accepts paying for it. More options in our full list of [Candy AI alternatives](/alternatives/candy-ai/).
