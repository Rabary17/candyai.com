## What is Secrets AI?

Secrets AI offers safe-for-work and adult chat with **500+ ready-made characters**, shown as animated cards you swipe through, plus a detailed creator: realistic or anime style, ethnicity, skin tone, hair, eyes, body type, age, personality, extra features and a choice of voices. Paid plans add image and video generation and voice calls. Payment by crypto is accepted.

## How this review was put together

We did not pay for Secrets AI ourselves for this version. We cross-checked **2026 video tests (transcripts read in full), user reviews summarised in those tests and on Trustpilot, and Secrets AI's own plan description**. Note that Secrets AI invites its customers to review it on Trustpilot, which tends to raise scores. Our [method](/how-we-test/) explains the score.

## Conversation and role-play

This is where Secrets AI shines. In one test, a character built as an enigmatic keeper of ancient knowledge who speaks in riddles **never broke character**, and when the user abruptly switched to an absurd new storyline, the AI wove it into the persona instead of resetting. Users quoted in reviews say the conversation flow beats Candy AI, OurDream AI and GirlfriendGPT, and praise its memory of past conversations.

It is not flawless: some users report occasional forgetfulness, repetition and unnecessary story changes, which a 2026 update set out to address.

## Images, video and voice

Reviewers rate image generation **hit-or-miss**: one user reported characters with three arms, and even fans of the chat say images are less consistent than on rival platforms. Video is the most-marketed feature but also the most expensive in Moments. Voice calls are included on Premium.

## Pricing and Moments

| Plan | Monthly | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 20 messages a day, 200 Moments at sign-up, 1 custom character |
| Lite | $5.99 | 100 messages a day, 1,000 Moments a month |
| Plus | $9.99 | Unlimited messages, 3,000 Moments a month |
| Premium | $19.99 ($13.33 a month yearly) | Unlimited messages, 8,000 Moments, images, video, voice calls, premium models |
| Ultimate | $39.99 | 15,000 Moments, group chats |

Moments pay for images, video and other media. Users like the size of the allowance on paid plans, but **unused Moments do not roll over** to the next month.

## Is Secrets AI safe?

Secrets AI is an active service whose developers are described as transparent and responsive on Discord. As with any companion app, chats are stored to power memory: keep real names, addresses and photos of real people out of them, and read the refund terms before paying, as they are not widely documented.

## Our verdict

**Secrets AI has one of the most flexible chat engines in the category**, and it is a strong pick if conversation and role-play matter more than pictures. The Moments system and inconsistent images keep it behind the leaders overall. Try the free plan, and if you subscribe, use your Moments each month.
