We are a small editorial team that tests AI companion apps with real accounts and our own money. AI companions are growing fast, and most reviews online are written without opening the app, or by the apps themselves. We wanted a place where prices are checked, limits are measured and verdicts can be traced back to a test.

## Our principles

- **Independent scores.** Commissions never change a rating or a ranking.
- **Prices checked monthly**, with the date shown on every page.
- **No explicit images.** Screenshots are cropped or blurred.
- **Adults only.** We write for people aged 18 and over.
- **Corrections.** Spotted an error? Write to us through the [contact page](/contact/); we fix verified mistakes within 72 hours.
