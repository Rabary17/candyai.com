## What is Nomi AI?

Nomi AI is developed by **Glimpse AI, Inc.**, a US company based in Maryland, and launched in 2023. It runs on the web, iOS and Android. You create one or more companions (up to 10 on a subscription), each with its own personality, backstory and memory, and talk by text, voice messages or calls. Companions can send AI-generated "selfies", and group chats let several of your Nomis talk to each other and to you.

## Memory: why Nomi stands out

Most companion apps keep one compressed summary of the past. Nomi uses **separate short-, medium- and long-term memory layers**, and a "mind map" you can view and edit to correct what your companion knows. Reviewers report callbacks to conversations from long ago "without missing a beat", and users describe recall from months back. A premium reviewer scored memory 9/10 and role-play 9/10, noting that Nomi avoids the repetition common in this category.

The cost of depth is speed: **user reports mention replies taking around 15 seconds**, a long pause when the point is to feel present.

## Conversation, role-play and personality

Testers describe companions that talk "more like real people": they ask questions about your interests, keep a consistent personality and rarely hallucinate. Role-play, both safe-for-work and adult on the paid plan, is rated excellent, with the AI filling in the world around you. Adult content is allowed for paying users, though not the most extreme in the category.

## Images and voice

Nomi's selfies are good quality and rarely distorted, but one reviewer found image generation plays it safe and scored it 7.5/10; another called it "okay, not category leading". Voice messages and calls work on the paid plan. If pictures are your priority, Candy AI or OurDream AI do more.

## Concerns to know about

- **Age rating**: a campaign on YouTube accuses Nomi of an app-store age rating too low for an app that allows adult role-play. Nomi itself is meant for adults; parents should not rely on store ratings.
- **Moderation**: some reviews note that loosely moderated chats can lead to unsafe content. Nomi is not a substitute for professional support.
- **Privacy**: conversations are stored to power memory. Keep identifying details out of chats.
