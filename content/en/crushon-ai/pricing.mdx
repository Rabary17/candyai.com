## CrushOn AI plans compared

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Messages | Main extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | Limited (see below) | Free model, ads, filtered characters only |
| Standard | $5.99 | $58.88 a year | 2,000 a month | Adult mode, better models, 16K memory, group chat |
| Premium | $14.99 | $94.88 a year | 6,000 a month | Top models, longer memory, more character slots |
| Deluxe | $49.90 | about $358.80 a year | Unlimited | Maximum memory, 100 character slots |

The free allowance moved in 2026 (100 messages a month in older reports, 50 a day in recent guides). Prices are those reported by CrushOn's plan page and several 2026 guides; promotions change often.

## Which plan do you need?

- **Occasional chats**: Free, or Standard yearly ($58.88) if you want adult mode.
- **Daily role-play, about 1 hour**: Premium yearly ($94.88) gives about 200 messages a day and better models.
- **Several hours a day**: Deluxe, unlimited, but it costs more than three times Premium.
