## Other ways to pay less

- **Pick annual billing** if you plan to stay more than a few months.

## Beware of fake codes and "free credits" generators

Sites that promise unlimited free credits or a premium unlock in exchange for a survey or a download are scams. They can steal your account or install malware. Real discounts only appear at checkout on the official site.
