## Is CrushOn AI legit?

Yes, in the sense that it is a real service that delivers what it sells: role-play with a huge library of characters. Reviewers describe it as community-driven and long-established. That does not make it risk-free.

## Privacy

Mozilla Foundation's *Privacy Not Included* review of romantic AI chatbots flagged CrushOn AI among apps with weak privacy practices. One reviewer also points out that the privacy policy allows chats to be **monitored for safety and appropriate content** and used to improve the service. Assume anything you type can be stored and read.

## Billing

Billing and payment-method complaints, including allegations of unauthorised charges, come up repeatedly in user reviews. **Use a card or payment method you can monitor**, note your renewal date, and cancel from your account well before it.

## Content

Characters are made by other users. Unfiltered mode is reserved for adults, and filtered mode is the default. Report characters that break the rules.
