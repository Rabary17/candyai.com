**Short answer: yes, CrushOn AI is free to use, with limits.** The free plan runs on the basic model with 8K memory, shows ads and only gives access to filtered characters. The message allowance changed in 2026: older reviews report **100 messages a month**, more recent guides **50 messages a day**. Check the counter in your account when you sign up.

## What the free plan includes

- The full character library in filtered (safe-for-work) mode.
- The free chat model, which one reviewer already found "very good".
- Up to 200 messages of history per character and 8K context memory.
- Daily rewards and tasks that add message credits.

## What it does not include

Adult (unfiltered) mode, the better chat models, longer memory, group chat and long-term history are on paid plans. **Free chat history is reported to be deleted after 7 days of inactivity**, so a story you leave for a week may be gone.

## When Standard is worth it

If you hit the cap often or want unfiltered role-play as a verified adult, Standard is the cheapest paid plan in the niche: $5.99 a month, or $58.88 a year. Compare all tiers on our [CrushOn AI pricing](/crushon-ai/pricing/) page.
