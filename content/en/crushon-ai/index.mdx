## What is CrushOn AI?

CrushOn AI hosts **hundreds of thousands of characters made by its community**, with a strong anime and cartoon tilt but plenty of realistic ones too. You browse by tag, pick a character and start chatting, or build your own in a detailed editor: name, age (adults only for romance), picture, scene card, greeting, personality, appearance and scenario, plus a visibility setting (public, unlisted or private) and a filtered or unfiltered mode.

It also has a creator economy: users can follow creators, comment on characters, share memories and tip creators with in-app diamonds. A "target play" mode turns a chat into a mini-game where you earn points by steering the character towards a goal.

## Chat quality, models and memory

This is CrushOn's strength. One reviewer found the free model "already very good", and the paid models noticeably richer, with longer, more in-character replies. You can **switch chat models**, adjust temperature, diversity and maximum reply length, regenerate or rate a reply, and **save up to 100 memories** per character so it remembers whatever you choose. Context memory grows with the plan, from 8K on free to 16K and more on paid tiers.

**Group chats** let several characters share one scene and bounce off each other in character. They run on a base model, so replies are a little less detailed than in one-to-one chats. Users quoted in reviews consistently say the bots "stay in character", which is the main reason people pay.

## What CrushOn AI does not do

CrushOn is essentially a chat and role-play site. Reviewers note **no meaningful image generation, video or AI phone calls**, unlike Candy AI or OurDream AI. Some 2026 guides mention voice messages and experimental images on higher tiers; treat those as extras rather than core features.

## Is CrushOn AI safe?

CrushOn AI is a working, long-running service, but it scores poorly on privacy. **Mozilla's *Privacy Not Included* review of romantic AI apps flagged it**, and one reviewer points out that the privacy policy allows chats to be monitored for safety and used to improve the service. Billing complaints, including disputed charges, come up repeatedly in user reviews. Use a card you can watch, check renewal dates, and never share real personal details. More on our [CrushOn AI safety](/crushon-ai/is-it-safe/) page.
