## How we rate AI girlfriend apps

Every app on this site goes through the same protocol: a paid account, a 15-minute conversation each day, image and voice requests, and a memory check after one week and one month. Five criteria earn a mark out of ten: conversation, memory, images, voice and value for money. **We log every credit or token we spend**, because the price on the sales page is rarely what you pay. The full grid is on our page about [how we test](/how-we-test/).

Affiliate commissions never change a score. Some apps below pay us when you subscribe, others pay nothing, and they all sit in the same table with the same columns.

## The best AI girlfriend apps by use

Candy AI lets you build a companion from scratch: face, body type, voice and personality. ## Is it safe to use an AI girlfriend app?

Stick to apps run by an identifiable company, with published terms and a clear way to cancel. **Never install an unofficial APK that promises free Premium**: those files are the most common source of malware in this niche. Assume that anything you type can be stored, and keep real names, addresses and photos of other people out of the chat.
