## How we ranked AI character chat apps

Each app ran the same three role-play scenarios for a week on a phone and, when available, on the web. We scored story quality, character consistency after 200 messages, free limits, filters, images and price, using the grid on our [how we test](/how-we-test/) page. **Apps without an identifiable company or with unofficial downloads were excluded.**

## 2. Character AI: best free app

Character AI runs on iOS, Android and web with unlimited free messages and an optional c.ai+ subscription (about $9.99 a month). The character library is the biggest of all. **The content filter is strict and open-ended chat is closed to under-18 users**, which pushes many adults to the [Character AI alternatives](/alternatives/).

## 7. Talkie and PolyBuzz

Both are free mobile apps with large libraries, voice replies and a young audience. Filters are strict and ads or premium prompts appear quickly. See our [Talkie AI alternatives](/alternatives/talkie-ai/) and [PolyBuzz alternatives](/alternatives/polybuzz/).

## iPhone, Android or web?

App Store and Google Play rules force apps to filter more than their web versions. If you want mature role-play as a verified adult, the web version of CrushOn AI, Joi AI, OurDream AI or Candy AI is usually the only option. **Never install an APK from outside the official stores to unlock features**: that is the most common way to get malware in this niche.
