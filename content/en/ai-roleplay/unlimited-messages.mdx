## What "unlimited" really means

- **Fair-use limits.** Some plans slow you down or switch to a smaller model after a high number of messages.
- **Memory is separate.** Unlimited messages do not mean the character remembers them all.
- **Extras are metered.** Images, voice and video almost always use credits.
