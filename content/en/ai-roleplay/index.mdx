An AI roleplay platform is a chat app built around characters. You pick a fantasy knight, a detective, a rival from school or a character someone else wrote, and the AI plays that role while you write the story together. Some platforms add images, voice or group chats. **What really separates them is the content filter, the memory, and how many free messages you get before a paywall.**

## How we tested AI roleplay platforms

We ran the same three scenarios on every platform: a fantasy adventure, a slow-burn romance and a mystery with several characters. Each session lasted 30 minutes, and we checked after 50 and 200 messages whether the character still knew who it was and what had happened. We scored story quality, character consistency, memory, filters and value for money, using the grid on our [how we test](/how-we-test/) page.

## The best AI roleplay platforms

### CrushOn AI

### Character AI

Character AI is the platform most people start with. Chat is free and unlimited, c.ai+ costs about $9.99 a month for faster replies, and the library counts millions of characters. **The filter blocks romantic and adult content, and since late 2025 users under 18 can no longer hold open-ended chats.** Many adults move on for those reasons; our list of [Character AI alternatives](/alternatives/) covers where they go.

### OurDream AI

OurDream AI mixes role-play and images: the character can show a scene as the story moves. <Todo>Test result in three sentences.</Todo> Details in our [OurDream AI review](/ourdream-ai/).

### Janitor AI

Janitor AI is popular for its open library and its option to plug in your own language model. That makes it flexible but also less predictable: quality depends on the model behind the character. <Todo>Test result, current free model, limits.</Todo> See our [Janitor AI alternatives](/alternatives/janitor-ai/).

### Nomi AI

Nomi AI is less a character library than a companion with excellent memory, but it handles role-play well and supports group chats. <Todo>Test result.</Todo> Read the [Nomi AI review](/nomi-ai/).

### Talkie and PolyBuzz

Both are mobile-first and free to start, with large catalogues of characters and many teenage users. Filters are strict and ads or paywalls come quickly. Adults looking for more freedom will find options in our pages on [Talkie AI alternatives](/alternatives/talkie-ai/) and [PolyBuzz alternatives](/alternatives/polybuzz/).

## How to choose an AI roleplay platform

| You want… | Pick |
|---|---|
| Free, unlimited, safe-for-work | Character AI |
| Mature stories, verified adults | CrushOn AI or Joi AI |
| Images inside the story | OurDream AI or Candy AI |
| One long story that stays coherent | Nomi AI |
| Your own model or API key | Janitor AI |
