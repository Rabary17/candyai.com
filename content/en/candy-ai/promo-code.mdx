## Is there a working Candy AI promo code?

We have not found a public Candy AI promo code that is confirmed to work. Most "codes" on coupon sites lead to the normal offer page. When it is missing, discounts apply automatically through the offer link or the plan length.

**We never publish a code we have not tested on the real checkout.** If a code appears here, it worked on the date shown in the box above.

## The real way to pay less: annual billing

The biggest, permanent saving is the plan length. Monthly costs $13.99. Annual drops to roughly $3.99–$6.30 a month, billed upfront. That is the same Premium with the same 100 monthly tokens. Our [Candy AI pricing breakdown](/candy-ai/pricing/) shows the math for three types of users.

## Seasonal offers to watch

Candy AI tends to push larger annual discounts around Black Friday, the holidays and events such as Valentine's Day, when it also runs token sales. We check the offer page weekly during those periods and update the box at the top.

## Watch out for fake coupon sites

Coupon aggregators often list "100% working" codes that were never valid, and some redirect to look-alike domains. **Never enter your card on a site that is not the official Candy AI checkout.** Fake "free token" tools are scams too, as we explain in [how Candy AI tokens work](/candy-ai/tokens/).
