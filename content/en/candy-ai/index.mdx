## What is Candy AI?

Candy AI is an adults-only AI companion platform launched in 2023. **EverAI Limited, a company registered in Malta, runs it**, and that name is what appears on your bank statement. You pick a ready-made character or design your own, then talk by text, voice notes or AI phone calls. The same character can send generated pictures and, since 2026, short "Live Action" video clips.

The pitch is a partner who remembers you and answers at 3 a.m. A large language model drives the conversation. A separate image model draws pictures in the character's style. Memory keeps the facts you share, such as your job, your city or your dog's name, and brings them back later.

Unlike Replika, Candy AI leans fully into romance and unlocks adult content on paid plans. That choice explains both its huge search volume and its strict 18+ rule. Our [complete guide to what Candy AI is](/candy-ai/) covers the features one by one.

## Is Candy AI safe and legit?

Candy AI is a real product from a registered European company, and cancelling works from the settings page. The risks lie elsewhere. **Your chats are stored on EverAI's servers**, so never share a real name, address or photo of a real person. The age check is a single checkbox, which matters for parents.

Billing shows "EverAI", not an adult brand, which many users appreciate. Payment runs through cards, crypto and, in some regions, PayPal. As an EU company, EverAI falls under GDPR, so you can ask for your data to be deleted. We detail each point in [is Candy AI safe and legit](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/).
