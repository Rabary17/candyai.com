## Who is behind Candy AI?

**EverAI Limited, a company registered in Malta under number C107181, owns and runs Candy AI.** Its name appears in the terms of service, the privacy notice and on every card charge. The corporate site, everai.ai, has no account area: Candy AI is the product, EverAI the company.

Being an EU company has two practical effects. First, GDPR applies, so you can request access to your data or its deletion. Second, payments run through mainstream processors, which explains why billing looks ordinary and discreet. The service launched in 2023 and grew fast, helped by a large affiliate programme and heavy advertising.

The age rule is clear on paper: users must be 18 or over. In practice the check is a checkbox at sign-up, a point we cover in [is Candy AI safe and legit](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/).

## How does Candy AI work?

You start by choosing a ready-made character or opening the creator. **The creator lets you set looks, voice, personality and relationship type** through menus and sliders. Once saved, the character opens a chat window and greets you.

Behind the screen, a language model writes replies in the character's voice and a memory layer stores key facts from your conversations. Ask for a picture and a separate image model draws the character in the scene you describe. Voice messages and phone calls use synthetic voices matched to the character.

Text chat counts as unlimited on Premium. Pictures, calls, voice notes and "Live Action" clips spend tokens from a monthly wallet. That split between free-flowing text and metered media drives almost every complaint and every saving tip you will read about the app.

## What is free and what costs money?

The free plan lets you create an account, browse characters and send a limited number of messages. **Images, calls and unlimited chat sit behind Premium**, which costs $13.99 billed monthly or far less on longer plans. Each plan includes 100 tokens a month; extra packs start around $9.99.

Most new users test the free tier for a day, then decide. Our page on [what Candy AI's free plan includes](/candy-ai/free/) lists the exact limits, and the [Candy AI pricing breakdown](/candy-ai/pricing/) simulates your real monthly bill.

## Who is Candy AI for?

Candy AI fits adults who want a romantic or flirty companion with a strong visual side. People who mainly want a supportive friend, or who cannot afford a variable monthly cost, often do better elsewhere. Teenagers should not use it at all.
