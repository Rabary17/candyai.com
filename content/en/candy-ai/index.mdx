## What is Candy AI?

Candy AI is an adults-only AI companion platform launched in 2023 and run by **EverAI Limited, a company registered in Malta**. You choose among more than a hundred ready-made characters (girlfriends, boyfriends and anime) or build your own with menus for looks, personality, voice and hobbies. You then talk by text, voice messages or AI phone calls, and the character can send generated pictures and short "Live Action" video clips.

The app runs in the browser on computer and phone. There is no native app in the official stores; on a phone you add the site to your home screen. Besides chat, you will find a discovery feed of AI-generated posts, a gallery of your images and an image generator driven by text prompts. Characters you create can stay private or be shared with other users.

## Conversation and memory

Reviewers agree on the first impression: **conversation is fluid, warm and quick to get going**, and the app feels like a finished consumer product rather than a chatbot wrapper. Characters stay flirty and responsive, which is exactly what most users come for.

Depth is the weak spot. The companion keeps profile facts such as your name, your interests and your role-play preferences across sessions, but several testers report that **in a long scene, past roughly 50 to 60 messages, it starts forgetting recent events or repeating the same affection**. That makes Candy AI great for short daily sessions and less convincing as a deep, months-long relationship. A simple test before paying: mention a small detail, change the subject, and ask about it ten minutes later.

Some users also report false positives from the automatic moderation, which can cut into an otherwise harmless conversation.

## Images, voice and Live Action video

Images are Candy AI's main draw. **Many testers rank its pictures among the most realistic in the niche**, and the character keeps a recognisable face more often than on cheaper rivals. Results are not perfect: users regularly mention odd hands, an extra limb or an image that does not match the prompt. Judge the generator on four or five requests, not on one lucky result.

Voice notes and calls work well for short exchanges, with low latency, but voices can sound flat in long calls. **Live Action video gives short clips of your character reacting to the chat**. Opinions are split: some users love them, others find movements unnatural, clips silent or too short for the tokens they cost.

## Candy AI pricing and the real monthly cost

<PriceCards />

Every Premium plan includes the same features: unlimited text chat, adult content, custom characters and **100 tokens a month**. Extra tokens come in packs from **$9.99 for 100 tokens up to $299.99 for 3,750**, which brings an image from about $0.40 down to about $0.08.

What a month really costs depends on media use:

- **Text-first user**: a few images a week fit in the 100 tokens. Total: the plan price only.
- **Regular user**: two or three images a day plus two short calls a week means around 400 tokens, so 300 to buy. Total: plan price plus $30 or more.
- **Video fan**: Live Action and private content packs (100 to 400 tokens each) can push the monthly bill well past $50.

The cheapest route is the yearly plan, plus a pack only in months when you want more pictures. Full breakdown and simulator on our [Candy AI pricing page](/candy-ai/pricing/).

## Is Candy AI safe and legit?

Candy AI is a real product from a registered EU company, and you can cancel from your account settings. **Payments show under a neutral merchant name**, not an adult brand, and you can pay by card or crypto. As an EU company, EverAI falls under GDPR, so you can ask for your data to be deleted.

The risks are elsewhere. Chats are stored on the company's servers to power memory and are not end-to-end encrypted, so **never share a real name, address or photo of a real person**. The age check relies on a declaration, which matters for parents. Unofficial "Candy AI mod APK" files are scams. Full checklist in [is Candy AI safe and legit](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/).

## Our verdict: is Candy AI worth it?

**Yes, if you know what you are paying for.** Candy AI is one of the easiest AI girlfriend apps to recommend as a first stop: the interface is polished, characters are attractive and responsive, and the images are among the best available. It is worth paying for when you mostly chat by text and enjoy a few pictures a week, ideally on the yearly plan.

It is a weaker choice if you expect a companion who remembers every detail of a long story, or if you plan to generate many images and videos: tokens then become the real subscription. Test the free messages, try three or four images on your first paid day, and check your token balance after a week before committing to a long plan.
