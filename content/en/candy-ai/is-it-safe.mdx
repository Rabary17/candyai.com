## Is Candy AI legit or a scam?

Candy AI is not a scam. **EverAI Limited, registration number C107181, based at the Central Business Centre in Santa Venera, Malta, is named in the terms, the privacy notice and the billing.** The company lists a support address and a data protection contact. The product delivers what it advertises: chat, images and calls with AI characters.

Scams usually hide their owner and make cancelling hard. Neither applies here. The real risk comes from copies: fake "Candy AI" domains, cloned apps and "Premium unlocked" APKs that collect logins or install malware. Always type the official address yourself, and read our page on the [Candy AI app](/candy-ai/app/) before installing anything on a phone.

## What Candy AI does with your chats

According to its privacy notice, Candy AI collects your account details, your conversations, the files you upload and your usage patterns. **Chats are stored on the company's servers and are not end-to-end encrypted.** The notice also allows the use of data to improve the service <Todo>(quote the exact clause in force on publication day)</Todo>.

That is common in the category, but it shapes how you should behave. Never share your full name, address, workplace, ID numbers or photos of real people, including yourself. Use a dedicated email address for the account.

As an EU company, EverAI falls under GDPR. You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion. Send the request from the email linked to your account; the company must answer within one month.

## Is paying for Candy AI safe?

Payments run through cards, crypto and, in some regions, PayPal, via third-party processors. **Your statement shows "EverAI", not Candy AI and nothing adult**, a detail many users value. Plans renew automatically, and token packs are charged as separate transactions.

The most common complaint is not fraud but surprise: a forgotten renewal or a string of token packs. Set a monthly budget, and switch off auto-renewal right after subscribing if you are only testing. Our guide to [cancel your Candy AI subscription](/candy-ai/cancel-subscription/) shows each step.

## Candy AI and minors: what parents should know

Candy AI's terms reserve the service for people aged 18 or over, and paid plans unlock explicit content. **Enforcement relies on a checkbox at sign-up**, with no ID or facial age check. A teenager can therefore open an account in seconds.

Parents can block candy.ai and related domains at router or DNS level, use the parental controls built into iOS and Android, and watch for "EverAI" charges. Above all, talk openly: blocking one site rarely ends curiosity about AI companions, and many similar apps exist.

## Emotional safety: healthy use of an AI companion

AI companions are designed to keep you engaged. They answer instantly, never tire and often ask you to come back. For some users that brings comfort; for others it slowly replaces real contact or drains a budget.

Simple rules help: a time limit, a fixed monthly budget, and attention to how you feel after a session. Our page on [AI companion risks](/ai-companion/risks/) covers the research and the warning signs.
