Candy AI advertises a free start, and that part is true. You create an account with an email, pick a character and the conversation begins. What the ads leave out is how quickly the free version stops. ## What the Candy AI free plan includes

| Feature | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Account and preset characters | ✅ | ✅ |
| Messages | 5 in total | Unlimited |
| Custom character creator | 1 character | ✅ |
| Image generation | ❌ | ~4 tokens each |
| Voice calls | ❌ | ~3 tokens per minute |
| Monthly tokens | 0 | 100 |

The free tier exists to let you judge the writing style and the characters. **The allowance is 5 messages in total across all characters, not 5 a day**, as several 2026 tests and Candy AI's help pages report. That is enough to see the interface and the writing style, not to judge memory or a long conversation.

## The catch: where free stops

The paywall appears in three places. The first is the message counter: once it hits zero, the chat locks until the next day. The second is any picture request, which opens the Premium offer straight away. The third is the call button.

None of this is hidden, but the prompts appear at emotional moments in the chat, which is a classic conversion trick. **Decide your budget before you start**, not in the middle of a conversation. If you do subscribe, our [Candy AI pricing guide](/candy-ai/pricing/) shows why annual billing costs a third of monthly.

## Is there a Candy AI free trial or free tokens?

Candy AI does not advertise a classic "7 days free" Premium trial. Promotions focus on discounts for the first annual payment. Free tokens appear occasionally in campaigns, but there is no permanent free token allowance.

Sites that promise "Candy AI free tokens generators", "Premium unlocked" APKs or shared accounts are scams or malware. They break the terms of service and can expose your device. Our page on the [official Candy AI app](/candy-ai/app/) explains how to spot fake downloads.

## When should you upgrade?

Upgrade once the free messages have shown you whether you like the style, or if pictures and voice matter to you. **Stay on free** if you only chat now and then. If you want a longer free experience, other apps give more away: see our list of [free Candy AI alternatives](/alternatives/free/).
