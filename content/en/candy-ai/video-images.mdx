## How the Candy AI image generator works

You ask for a picture in the chat or open the generator menu. **You describe the scene, outfit and pose, and the model renders your character in that context.** The image arrives in the conversation within seconds. Premium users can generate adult content; this site only shows safe-for-work samples.

## Image quality: what testers report

Most 2026 reviews agree that **Candy AI produces some of the most realistic companion images available**, in a realistic or anime style, and that the character keeps a recognisable face more often than on cheaper rivals. The failures are the usual ones for image models: hands with the wrong number of fingers, an extra arm, or a picture that ignores part of the prompt. Users on Trustpilot mention these misses regularly, so expect to regenerate now and then, and remember that each try costs about 4 tokens.

The practical advice from testers is the same: judge the generator on four or five requests with the same character, not on one lucky result. Short, concrete prompts (place, outfit, pose, mood) give the most consistent results.

## Live Action video: what to expect

Live Action, added in 2026, turns your character into a short animated clip. Clips last a few seconds and show the character moving or reacting to the chat; a one-minute video costs about 15–20 tokens, and turning an existing image into a clip about 12. Opinions are split: some users praise the realism, others find movements unnatural or clips silent, and several say video is not worth the tokens compared with pictures. Video spends tokens much faster than pictures, so a few clips can empty the monthly wallet. Our page on [how Candy AI tokens work](/candy-ai/tokens/) lists the rates.

## Tips for better images with fewer tokens

- Name the setting, clothing, pose and lighting in one prompt.
- Keep the same art style as the character's profile.
- Avoid conflicting instructions, which cause re-rolls.
- Save favourites rather than regenerating similar scenes.
