## Candy AI plans compared

Candy AI sells **one Premium tier with three billing lengths**: monthly at $13.99, quarterly at about $8.99 a month, and annual at roughly $3.99 to $6.30 a month. Features do not change between them. You get unlimited text chat, custom characters, adult content and 100 tokens every month.

The free plan covers account creation, preset characters and a small daily message allowance. It has no image generation and no calls. Prices move with region, currency and promotions, so European visitors often see euro amounts and seasonal discounts on the annual plan. We recheck the figures every month; the date sits at the top of this page.

Payment works by Visa, Mastercard or crypto. **The charge appears under a neutral merchant name linked to EverAI**, the Maltese company behind the app, with no adult wording.

## How Candy AI tokens work

| Action | Approx. token cost |
|---|---|
| Text message | 0 (unlimited on Premium) |
| Generated image | ~4 tokens |
| Voice call | ~3 tokens per minute |
| Listening to a voice message | a few tokens (shown before playing) |
| Create a custom character | about 10 tokens |
| Turn an image into a video | about 12 tokens |
| Live Action clip (up to 60 s) | about 15–20 tokens |
| Private content pack | 100–400 tokens |

Tokens are the app's second currency. Premium drops 100 into your wallet each month; when they run out, packs start around $9.99 for 100 tokens and get cheaper per unit as they grow. A 10-minute call therefore burns 30 tokens, close to a third of the monthly allowance.

Several sources report that unused monthly tokens expire at the end of each billing cycle, so plan as if they do not roll over. Our page on [how Candy AI tokens work](/candy-ai/tokens/) lists every cost and a few ways to stretch them.

## How to pay less for Candy AI

- **Test the free plan first**, so you only commit once you know you like it. Our page on [whether Candy AI is free](/candy-ai/free/) lists its limits.
- **Pick annual billing** once you are sure: it is the single biggest saving.
- **Describe images precisely.** A vague prompt leads to re-rolls, and each re-roll costs 4 tokens.
- **Buy larger packs only if you use them every month**; otherwise stick to the smallest.
- **Check current offers** before you pay on our [Candy AI promo code](/candy-ai/promo-code/) page.

## Is Candy AI worth the price?

For text-first users on an annual plan, Candy AI costs less than a streaming subscription and delivers a polished experience. Value drops for people who live in the image generator: at that point the token packs dominate the bill, and rivals with bundled image credits can work out cheaper.

Replika charges about $19.99 a month for Pro, or $69.99 a year, for a text-and-voice friendship experience without image generation. OurDream AI and CrushOn AI also mix a subscription with credits for media; compare their plans before assuming one is cheaper. If cost matters most, compare our list of [Candy AI alternatives](/alternatives/candy-ai/) before you subscribe.

## Cancelling and refunds

You can switch off auto-renewal at any time in your account settings. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. Refunds are not guaranteed: read the refund section of the terms before you pay. Step-by-step screenshots sit on our guide to [cancel your Candy AI subscription](/candy-ai/cancel-subscription/).
