> **Short answer:** log in, open your profile or account settings, go to Subscription, and turn off auto-renewal. You keep Premium until the end of the current billing period. No email or call is needed.

Menu names change from time to time. If you cannot find the option, Candy AI support and its Discord ticket system can process the cancellation.

## Cancel when you paid by crypto or a third-party processor

If your payment went through a third-party processor, check its account page for a recurring payment as well. **Do this in addition to cancelling in Candy AI**, not instead, so the account status stays clear.

Crypto payments do not renew automatically in the same way; check your plan status in settings.

## What happens after you cancel?

Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. After that, the account falls back to the free plan with its message cap. **Unused tokens:** monthly tokens are reported to expire at the end of each billing cycle, so use them before the plan ends. Your characters and chat history stay in the account unless you delete it.

## Can you get a refund from Candy AI?

Refund rules depend on the terms of service in force and on local consumer law, so read the refund section before you pay. In practice, contact support quickly after an unwanted renewal, explain the situation and include the transaction date. Keep the "EverAI" line from your statement as proof.

If you were charged for a plan you never used, you can also contact your card issuer, but try the merchant first.

## Delete your Candy AI account and data

Cancelling stops billing; it does not erase anything. To remove your data, use the delete account option in settings or send a GDPR deletion request from your account email. Our page on [whether Candy AI is safe](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/) explains what the company stores.
