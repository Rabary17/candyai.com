## Cancel when you paid through PayPal or an app store

If you subscribed through PayPal, you can also stop the recurring payment from PayPal's automatic payments settings. **Do this in addition to cancelling in Candy AI**, not instead, so the account status stays clear. If a mobile store handled the payment <Todo>(confirm if applicable)</Todo>, cancel in the store's subscriptions menu.

Crypto payments do not renew automatically in the same way; check your plan status in settings.

## What happens after you cancel?

Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. After that, the account falls back to the free plan with its message cap. **Unused tokens:** <Todo>state whether they remain usable after cancellation</Todo>. Your characters and chat history stay in the account unless you delete it.

## Can you get a refund from Candy AI?

Refund rules depend on the terms of service in force and on local consumer law <Todo>(quote current terms)</Todo>. In practice, contact support quickly after an unwanted renewal, explain the situation and include the transaction date. Keep the "EverAI" line from your statement as proof.

If you were charged for a plan you never used, you can also contact your card issuer, but try the merchant first.

## Delete your Candy AI account and data

Cancelling stops billing; it does not erase anything. To remove your data, use the delete account option in settings or send a GDPR deletion request from your account email. Our page on [whether Candy AI is safe](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/) explains what the company stores.
