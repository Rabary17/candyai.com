## Is there an official Candy AI app?

App stores restrict adult content, which limits what an official mobile app can offer. As a result, **the complete Candy AI experience lives on the website**, which adapts to phone screens. Several listings on Google Play and the App Store use similar names; many belong to other developers <Todo>(list the official listing if one exists on publication day)</Todo>.

Before downloading anything, check the developer name. The company behind Candy AI is EverAI Limited. A listing under another name is not the official product.

## The risk of Candy AI APKs and "mod" versions

Searches for "Candy AI mod APK" and "unlimited messages APK" are common, and so are the sites that answer them. **These files are not made by EverAI.** At best they show a copy of the website; at worst they install adware, steal saved passwords or capture your Candy AI login and card details.

They also break the terms of service, so any account linked to them can be closed. Premium features run on EverAI's servers, which means no APK can unlock them for free. Our guide on [whether Candy AI is safe](/candy-ai/is-it-safe/) lists other red flags.
