## Five ways to make tokens last

- **Write detailed image prompts**: setting, outfit, pose, lighting. Fewer re-rolls, fewer tokens.
- **Keep calls short** and switch to text for long conversations.
- **Batch picture requests** on the days you really want them.
- **Avoid big packs** unless you empty them every month.
- **Check offers** on our [Candy AI promo code](/candy-ai/promo-code/) page before topping up.

## Are free tokens real?

Candy AI sometimes adds tokens in promotions, but "free token generators" and shared accounts are scams. They cannot create tokens, which live on EverAI's servers, and they often aim at your login. See the image side of the app on our [Candy AI images and videos](/candy-ai/video-images/) page.
