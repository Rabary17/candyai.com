## What Candy AI tokens are used for

| Action | Token cost* |
|---|---|
| Text chat | 0 on Premium |
| Image | ~4 |
| Voice call | ~3 per minute |
| Listening to a voice message | a few tokens |
| Create a custom character | about 10 |
| Turn an image into a video | about 12 |
| Live Action clip (up to 60 s) | about 15–20 |
| Private content pack | 100–400 |

*Costs reported by several 2026 tests and Candy AI's help pages, checked November 2026. The app shows the exact cost before each action.

Tokens are Candy AI's internal currency. **Premium covers unlimited text, while every generated media file draws from your wallet.** The split keeps the base price low but makes heavy image users pay more.

## Token packs and prices

When the monthly 100 tokens run out, you can buy packs. Prices start around $9.99 for 100 tokens and fall per unit on bigger bundles, up to $299.99 for 3,750 tokens. On the largest pack an image works out at about $0.08, against about $0.40 with the smallest. Each pack appears as a separate "EverAI" charge.

Unused monthly tokens are reported to expire at the end of each billing cycle, so treat the 100 included tokens as use-it-or-lose-it and check your balance before buying a large pack. Our [Candy AI pricing breakdown](/candy-ai/pricing/) puts packs in context with the plans.

## Five ways to make tokens last

- **Write detailed image prompts**: setting, outfit, pose, lighting. Fewer re-rolls, fewer tokens.
- **Keep calls short** and switch to text for long conversations.
- **Batch picture requests** on the days you really want them.
- **Avoid big packs** unless you empty them every month.
- **Check offers** on our [Candy AI promo code](/candy-ai/promo-code/) page before topping up.

## Are free tokens real?

Candy AI sometimes adds tokens in promotions, but "free token generators" and shared accounts are scams. They cannot create tokens, which live on EverAI's servers, and they often aim at your login. See the image side of the app on our [Candy AI images and videos](/candy-ai/video-images/) page.
