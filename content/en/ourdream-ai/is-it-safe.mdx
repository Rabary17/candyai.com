## Is OurDream AI legit?

Yes. OurDream AI names **Dream Studio USA, Inc.** as its operator and data controller, with a European entity, **TekTopia Ltd**, registered in Cyprus. It runs a public Trust & Safety hub explaining what is allowed, how moderation works and how to report problems. Users in reviews and on Reddit describe a working service; complaints focus on coin costs, not on fraud.

## Content rules

OurDream's rules block the creation of underage characters, prohibit deepfakes and celebrity look-alikes, and forbid non-consensual scenarios. The platform is for adults only.

## What happens to your data

According to OurDream and several safety reviews, data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and the company says it does not sell your data or chats. **One reviewer points out that the privacy policy still allows user data to be used to improve its AI models**, so treat chats as stored and not fully private. You can contact the trust team to request deletion.

## Billing and cancellation

Charges appear as **"Dream Studio"**, which the terms disclose. Plans renew automatically: turn off renewal in your account settings if you only want one period, and check the refund section of the terms before you pay.
