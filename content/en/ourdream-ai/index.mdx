## What is OurDream AI?

OurDream AI is an adults-only AI companion and role-play platform launched in 2025. It is run by **Dream Studio USA, Inc.**, with a European entity, TekTopia Ltd, registered in Cyprus. You pick from a large library of ready-made and user-made characters or build your own (looks, personality, backstory), then chat, ask for images, place voice calls or generate short video clips. Group chats let several characters join one scene.

The service runs mainly in the browser. Reports differ on mobile apps, and several testers list the lack of a full native app as a drawback.

## Conversation and memory

Memory is where OurDream earned its reputation. In one test, details planted early in a conversation (a favourite film, an invented job) were still remembered many messages later, **noticeably better than several cheaper rivals**, with only occasional slips in long sessions. Another reviewer said the companion "remembers you" in a way that makes chats feel less like a reset each time. Users on Reddit, as quoted in reviews, agree that memory and customisation are its strongest points compared with Candy AI or CrushOn AI.

Memory also scales with the plan: Deluxe advertises three times the chat memory of Premium. Conversation quality depends on how clearly you set up the scene; testers advise giving the character a clear setup rather than judging the app on one lazy message.

## Images, video and voice

Image quality is described as consistently solid, with several image engines to choose from. **Video is the stand-out feature**: OurDream is one of the few companion apps generating clips at all. It is still in beta, though, and one tester saw roughly six successful clips out of ten attempts. Voice calls work smoothly with natural pacing.

## Pricing and real monthly cost

| Plan | Price | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 50 messages a day, 5 images a day, 55 coins at sign-up, partial adult content, no video or voice |
| Premium monthly | $19.99 a month | Unlimited messages, 1,000 coins a month, images, video, voice |
| Premium yearly | $9.99 a month ($119.88 a year) | Same as Premium, plus a one-time 1,000-coin bonus |
| Deluxe | $59.99 a month | 5,000 coins a month, better chat models, about 3× memory |

Extra coins are sold in bundles, from about $11.99 for 1,000. **A text-first user fits easily in Premium**; someone generating ten or more video clips a month will need several thousand coins, which puts the real bill at $45 or more. ## Is OurDream AI safe?

OurDream AI is a legitimate service from registered companies, with a public Trust & Safety hub and a contact address for the trust team. Charges appear as **"Dream Studio"** on your statement. Its rules block underage characters, deepfakes and celebrity look-alikes. The privacy picture is mixed: the company states that data is encrypted and not sold, but one reviewer notes that the privacy policy allows user data to be used to improve its AI models. Keep real names, addresses and photos of real people out of your chats. More on our [OurDream AI safety](/ourdream-ai/is-it-safe/) page.

## Our verdict

**OurDream AI is one of the strongest all-round companion platforms of 2026**, and the best pick when memory and story matter more than polish. The free plan is generous enough to judge it properly. Pay yearly if you stay: the monthly price is hard to justify. Watch your coin balance if you get into video, which is where costs climb and where the feature is still maturing.
