## What the free plan includes

You sign up with an email, choose a ready-made character or build your own, and chat. The 50 daily messages are enough for a real conversation and to test the memory that OurDream is known for. The 5 daily images use OurDream's image engines at no coin cost, and the free allowance also continues on paid plans.

## What free users cannot do

- **Video clips** and **voice calls** need Premium.
- **Adult content** is only partly available.
- **Unlimited messages** and the monthly 1,000 coins come with Premium.

## How DreamCoins work

DreamCoins are OurDream's in-app currency. Reported costs in 2026: about 5–10 coins per image beyond the free ones, about 50 coins a minute for voice, and 100–300 coins per video clip. The 55 sign-up coins are a taste, not a budget. Details on our [OurDream AI pricing](/ourdream-ai/pricing/) page.

## When paying makes sense

Upgrade if you keep hitting the 50-message cap, or if video and voice are what you came for. The yearly plan at $9.99 a month is half the monthly price. Compare with Candy AI, whose free plan is only 5 messages in total but whose yearly plan is cheaper: see [Candy AI vs OurDream AI](/vs/candy-ai-vs-ourdream-ai/).

No. Sites promising unlimited free coins or a premium APK are scams that aim at your account or your device. Use the official site only.
