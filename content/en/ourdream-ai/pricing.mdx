## How DreamCoins work

| Action | DreamCoins (reported) |
|---|---|
| Image beyond the 5 free daily ones | about 5–10 |
| Voice call | about 50 a minute |
| Video clip (5–30 s) | 100–300 |

The coin price shows before each action. Extra bundles start around $11.99 for 1,000 coins and get cheaper per coin in larger packs.

## What different users really spend

- **Text-first**: unlimited chat plus the 5 free images a day. Premium yearly: **$9.99 a month**, coins barely touched.
- **Regular**: a few voice calls and two or three videos a week, around 1,500–2,000 coins a month. Premium plus one bundle: **about $20–30**.
- **Video fan**: ten or more clips a month and long calls, 4,000+ coins. **$45 or more**, or Deluxe.
