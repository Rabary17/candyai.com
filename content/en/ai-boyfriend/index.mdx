## How we tested AI boyfriend apps

We created a boyfriend on each app and ran the same daily script for two weeks: a check-in about the day, a light argument, a request for advice and a voice exchange when available. We scored warmth, consistency, memory, voice and value, using our [test protocol](/how-we-test/).

## Before you get attached

An AI boyfriend can be good company, but it is a product designed to keep you engaged. Set limits on time and money, keep personal details private, and watch for signs that the app is replacing people rather than adding to your life. Our guide to the [risks of AI companions](/ai-companion/risks/) covers this in more depth.
